## What changed since you went to bed

### Themes are now visual-only (decision 102)
- **What theme CSS may set:** colours, backgrounds, borders, corners, shadows, font family, weight and style, underlines,
  and opacity.
- **What it can't do:** anything that moves, sizes, clips, hides, animates or changes text spacing is dropped by the
  Customizer and refused on import, with the reason shown.
- **Layout:** it comes from the theme's options (navigation side, width, menu, Now arrangement), which Score renders
  itself.
- **Side effect:** Ledger and Studio lost their letter-spacing and line-height tweaks.

### Consent screens are protected
A theme can't hide, fade, cover, clip or blank any of these:
- approvals, answers and the task drawer's decisions;
- the add-coding-agent form;
- the start-orchestration and start-workflow forms (approval policy, spend confirmations, what each coding agent can
  reach);
- MCP access;
- Settings › Remote access (password, pairing, paired browsers, passkeys) and the pair page;
- the Settings link and every way back.

Each decision is checked as a whole when you act on it. If anything is wrong, Score refuses, switches the theme off and
says nothing was sent. These parts also always read in Score's own fonts, and agent glyphs and Needs-you dots keep a
contrasting plate or edge under a theme.

### Ways back from a broken theme
- `?look=safe`
- Ctrl+Shift+0
- the command palette, which now also works in the chat-first view and the Customizer
- Settings › Look › Reset
- `scripts/score look reset`, which also clears each device's own light/dark choice

### Theme packages
- The browser and the server check packages with one shared rule set.
- Writes are conditional, so two editors can't silently overwrite each other.
- Swaps are journaled and recoverable, with fsync ordering for power loss.
- Pictures and fonts are tied to the theme's revision, so a stale window can't mix two versions.
- Saving the theme in use updates other open devices.

### Customizer
- Paper, Night shift and Dense are full themes you can "Start from".
- Pictures and fonts save in place.
- "Save as new" keeps your version after a conflict.
- Import → Replace checks the version it showed you.

### Cleanup
- Watch nests sub-agent steps by their real parent.
- Knowledge keeps "Save to history again" until a note really is in history, and treats a Git failure as "not saved"
  instead of "fine".
- Chat keeps the newest lines in view on load.
- Dark mode: the "Start plan" button was white on near-white; that's fixed.
- A personality draft survives a settings refresh, with "Reload theirs / Overwrite with mine" if someone changed it.
- A failed quota refresh no longer holds work.
