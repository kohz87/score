C, final: what gets built
C, fitted to the app’s rules: every body inside the glyph box, and any two shapes differing in at least 15% of their area, so agents stay tellable apart by shape at small sizes. So the pebble is squarer, the clover and flower more open, the bean’s dent shallower.
