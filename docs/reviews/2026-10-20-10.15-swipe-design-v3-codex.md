V3 still has P1 gaps. It addresses v2 findings **#3 and #5–7** at the design level; **#1–2 remain incomplete**, and **#4 needs a completion rule**.

1. **P1 — R2 does not atomically pin the turn Score validated.**  
   Design: [R2](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:114).

   Hermes’s drain lock is a [process-local `RLock`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/session_transcript.py:106). Direct [`append_message`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:404) calls bypass it. Meanwhile, [`rewind_user_turn`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_rewind.py:65) captures its **own later** active-row snapshot; it accepts neither the validated UID nor the caller’s expected active IDs.

   **Failure:** R2 validates A, another writer appends a user turn, then `rewind_user_turn(-1)` snapshots and removes that new turn. Its internal checks succeed. Score retires A although A remains in Hermes.

   **Change:** Pass the validated snapshot into the mutation. For this restricted, non-composite case, Hermes’s existing [`rewind_to_message`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:1826) accepts a physical target, `expected_active_ids` and expected target content, checked inside its transaction. Resolve those from the validated manifest in `hermes_private.py`, or require an upstream extension to `rewind_user_turn`. Holding the drain lock alone is insufficient.

2. **P1 — R1 cannot prove the pre-turn context survived, and R4 fences only Score’s room.**  
   Design: [R1’s reconciliation rules](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:102), [R4](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:129).

   **Failure:** Score rewinds A and crashes before recording success. While Score is down, another Hermes surface undoes the preceding turn too. On restart, A’s rows are archived and “nothing newer is live,” so R1 declares `rewound` and generates B from an incomplete prefix. Alternatively, another surface adds work between rewind and dispatch; B then receives extra context despite `context: 'same'`. Continued outside sessions are explicitly [shared with another surface](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/routing-dispatch.ts:145).

   **Change:** Persist the complete expected source and pre-turn baseline manifests, including payload digests and resolved lineage. Reconcile **exact** source/baseline states. Require session-wide exclusion or atomic expected-baseline validation at admission; refuse shared sessions where Hermes cannot provide it. Also exclude already-admitted compactions, which need not appear as nonterminal `message_runs`.

3. **P1 — A terminal run status does not establish a reconciled replacement transcript.**  
   Design: [Durability step 3](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:54), [R1’s `dispatched` recovery](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:109), [R5](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:135).

   **Failure:** B finishes in Hermes while Score is offline. Its run record later expires, and the transcript is temporarily unreadable during recovery. Existing recovery [treats unreadable history as absent and ends the run interrupted](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/hermes/live/runs.ts:110). Following v3, Score finalizes a failed alternative and releases its hold although Hermes may retain B’s complete answer.

   **Change:** Add a verified transcript checkpoint before finalizing the operation: input never persisted, identified partial attempt, identified completed attempt, or unknown. Unknown must remain `unresolved`. R5’s membership accounting and the “agent remembers n” notice must use that evidence. Commit visibility, membership, terminal operation state and hold release together. “Release” also needs a persisted adjudicated outcome and fencing of outstanding effects.

4. **P2 — R1 still lacks the lost-admission outcome rules.**  
   Design: [R1’s adoption of an existing intent](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:109).

   **Failure:** Hermes accepts B, but Score loses the response before obtaining its run ID. The operation remains `rewound`, with a `sent`/`unknown` intent. After a long restart, the existing intent path [refuses replay after 23 hours](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/hermes/live/intents.ts:89). There is no run handle for normal run recovery, and R1 specifies only dispatch/adoption.

   **Change:** Define transitions for every linked intent state, including definite admission refusal, acceptance without a recovered handle, and expired replay windows. Unprovable acceptance becomes `unresolved`; never mint another admission ID. Specify how a definite refusal creates the failed alternative and completes the operation.

5. **P2 — Forget needs its own recovery branch.**  
   Design: [R1](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:102), [R3](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:124).

   **Failure:** A synced delete reaches `rewound`, then Score crashes before creating its room version. R1’s only specified recovery from `rewound` dispatches the admission again. Applied to `kind: 'forget'`, that re-executes the question instead of finishing deletion.

   **Change:** Give each kind an explicit state machine. Forget recovery must atomically create/adopt the deletion version, retire transcript membership, mark `done` and release the hold. It must never enter regeneration dispatch. Calling existing 5.33 does not supply this missing journal branch.

6. **P2 — R4 blocks the replacement’s own normal hand-off completion.**  
   Design: [R4](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:129).

   **Failure:** B completes with an eligible `@reviewer` hand-off. [`completeRunMessage`](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/message-runs.ts:674) reserves that hand-off before completing the message. R4 requires reservations to return `409 held`, while the operation retains its hold until completion. Implementing both literally prevents completion.

   **Change:** Stage successor work until the operation’s final transaction, then publish the alternative, reserve its legitimate successors, finish the operation and release the hold atomically. Preserve hand-off limits and causal links; do not silently suppress the hand-off.

Direct answers to the adversarial-review questions:

- **Is latest causal work complete and serialized?** Mostly for Score-side admissions. R4 names the previously missing mutations and permanent plan consumption. Already-admitted compactions, external session writers and the replacement’s completion-generated work still need the rules above.
- **Is the journal sufficient across restarts?** No. Non-expiring holds and stable identities are sound, but exact baseline reconciliation, admission uncertainty, verified terminal outcomes, forget recovery and Release semantics remain incomplete.
- **Does 5.33 supply the guarantees, and are the checks available?** Its underlying mutation supplies useful transactional guards; its existing orchestration is insufficient. Raw/repaired conversation reads, UIDs, inactive rows and active physical IDs exist. The named `rewind_user_turn` cannot accept R2’s expected state. Pending queues and reroutes are inspectable locally, but there is no complete read-only pending-spool query: `_spooled_drop_sessions` is only a hint, and [`drain_transcript_spool`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/shutdown_flush.py:135) mutates files and can return zero on scan failure. R2’s unavailable-on-unknown rule must apply.
- **Is visibility independent of deletes and versions?** Yes at the design level. R7 closes the normal-read, FTS and frozen-share gaps; historical readers must apply their stated separate predicates. The requested integration tests are appropriate.

Read-only older alternatives with an explicit memory notice are sound.  
R5–R7 provide sound accounting, admission-material and visibility requirements.  
Preserving executions, approvals, costs, Watch history and persistent tool/memory effects is correct.  
Keeping private calls in `hermes_private.py` and mutations inside Hermes-owned APIs respects both boundaries.
