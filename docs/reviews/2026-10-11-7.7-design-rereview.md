The additional findings below include new design defects and false W assumptions on which v2 depends.

1. **N1 · P1 — A restored database can lose released work without triggering the journal fence.**

   **Locations:** §§4/8; [journal.ts:28](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/journal.ts:28), [fence.ts:173](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/fence.ts:173), [fence.ts:203](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/fence.ts:203).

   **Failure:** Take a database snapshot after `run_handle` commits but before release. Release the worker, then restore that snapshot while the group survives. The snapshot has the same dispatch generation and handle, so existing verification finds no mismatch. It lacks the process identity, `released_at`, and coding charges. §6 then calls this “didn’t start,” permitting retry alongside surviving work and restoring spent allowance.

   **Fix:** Add a fsync’d, generation-qualified release witness outside `$SCORE_HOME`, containing launch identity, policy hash, process identity, reservation and expiry. Extend verification, replay and compaction. A lost witnessed release must fence admission and preserve stop/accounting obligations. Admission-time journaling alone cannot witness later release or settlement.

2. **N2 · P2 — `released_at` cannot mean both durable registration and proven execution.**

   **Locations:** §4, especially lines 43–51; [process.ts:191](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:191), [database.ts:57](apps/server/src/db/database.ts:57).

   **Failure:** Commit the process row and `released_at`, then crash before authorization or the byte write; the CLI never ran, but recovery charges a failed try. Writing the marker after the byte creates the inverse unsafe window. Launcher death, `EPIPE`, or a buffered byte also breaks “present ⇒ executed.” SQLite and IPC cannot commit atomically. WAL with `synchronous=NORMAL` additionally permits transaction loss after system/power failure, although application crashes preserve committed transactions. [SQLite documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/pragma.html#pragma_synchronous).

   **Fix:** Specify distinct registered, release-intent, execution-confirmed and proven-no-launch phases. Release intent means **execution may have happened**. Bind authorization and debits to that transition; refund/reuse only with qualifying no-launch proof. Specify control descriptor ownership, byte validation, EOF/error handling and acknowledgements. Keep prompt stdin separate: the current helper writes it before recording. Node’s stdio streams also must not be treated as reopenable Unix pipe paths. [Node documentation](https://nodejs.org/docs/latest-v24.x/api/child_process.html#optionsstdio).

3. **N3 · P1 — The launcher’s child/group and death contract is missing.**

   **Locations:** §4; [process.ts:89](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:89), [process.ts:196](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:196), [process.ts:254](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:254).

   **Failure:** Reusing `spawnCli` inside the launcher creates another detached group. Killing or observing the launcher’s group then says nothing about the CLI. Conversely, a launcher that exits after spawning a same-group child triggers the existing reaper, which kills the healthy CLI after the grace period. Unexpected launcher death also loses its exit/result forwarding and any timer it owns.

   **Fix:** Specify a supervising launcher that remains alive, starts the CLI **without detaching**, keeps control descriptors out of CLI inheritance, forwards output and records the child’s exit disposition. Unexpected launcher death must retain obligations until qualifying job/group proof arrives. Test death before go, after go, during child creation and while descendants survive.

   Group proof must remain explicitly scoped: detached descendants and external MCP/background jobs can survive it. 7.5 accepts that residual risk; excluding Antigravity alone does not establish stronger quiescence for Claude/Codex.

4. **N4 · P1 — W still signals an unrelated replacement group when its replacement leader has exited.**

   **Locations:** v2’s opening claims and §6; [proc.ts:102](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/proc.ts:102), [proc.ts:129](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/proc.ts:129), [proc.ts:159](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/proc.ts:159).

   **Failure:** Original group 500 empties. A replacement leader reuses PID/PGID 500, starts member 510, then exits and is reaped. Recovery finds no holder of PID 500 and assumes member 510 belongs to the old group. A mocked process-table probe reproduced SIGTERM to that unrelated member. Matching boot identity does not prove uninterrupted group ownership.

   **Fix:** Remove the leader-absent ownership inference. Require a stable job identity or demonstrable ownership continuity; otherwise return `unobservable`. PID-directed signaling also needs identity-safe targets rather than unchecked numbers from an earlier scan.

5. **N5 · P1 — W conflates unreadable process state with absence.**

   **Locations:** §6; [proc.ts:4](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/proc.ts:4), [proc.ts:115](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/proc.ts:115), [proc.ts:130](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/proc.ts:130), [process.ts:128](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:128).

   **Failure:** Listing `/proc` succeeds but relevant stat reads fail. Every failure becomes `null`; recovery skips those processes and returns `gone`. A probe reproduced this outcome. There is also a scan race: a member forks after the PID listing and exits before its stat read, leaving a surviving child absent from the snapshot. The live reaper can similarly override a successful group-existence check with an empty scan.

   **Fix:** Distinguish confirmed disappearance from unreadable/malformed observations. An incomplete scan cannot establish absence. Use a qualified OS job/group observation protocol; inconclusive visibility must yield `unobservable`, preserve records and fence admission.

6. **N6 · P2 — Synchronous `authorise()` lacks a safe transaction and ordering contract.**

   **Locations:** §§4/8; [executor.ts:404](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:404), [ingest.ts:303](apps/server/src/hub/ingest.ts:303), [database.ts:21](apps/server/src/db/database.ts:21), [executor.ts:1862](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:1862).

   **Failure:** Existing executor mutations enqueue asynchronous ingest work. They cannot synchronously commit a debit through this callback. Calling `db.read()` inside the registration transaction throws a nested-transaction error; synchronously waiting for a queued writer while holding that writer cannot progress. Sending go inside a transaction instead permits execution before rollback.

   The runtime also queues execution before returning its handle. Current awaits generally let `recordHandle()` win, but the port supplies no guarantee that the attempt becomes `running` before authorization.

   **Fix:** Define a pure synchronous guard operating on the current transaction context. Commit a single-use, generation-qualified release transition, journal witness and debits together; issue go through an explicit post-commit effect without yielding. Define Stop/release ordering and a committed “armed” handshake. There is no implemented callback to diagnose today; this is a missing integration protocol.

7. **N7 · P1 — Final authorization omits admission conditions that can change while queued.**

   **Locations:** §4; [executor.ts:1909](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:1909), [executor.ts:1940](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:1940), [executor.ts:1992](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:1992), [decisions.ts:136](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/decisions.ts:136).

   **Failure:** A turn waits behind Chat. The person lowers its dollar budget, revokes unknown-spend acceptance, pauses the agent/team, or withdraws consumed input. These facts can change without the run already having a paused status. §4’s listed checks still authorize execution because coding minutes remain available and the attempt remains `running`.

   **Fix:** Extract and reuse the complete final admission predicate, including current dollar accounting, scoped uncertainty acceptance, independent holds/fences, input eligibility, mapping/gate validity, `retired` and owed Stop. Read it at the release transaction, not from derived status or cached state.

8. **N8 · P1 — Comparing current policy does not bind the prepared invocation to frozen policy.**

   **Locations:** §§2/4; [service.ts:569](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:569), [service.ts:594](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:594), [service.ts:659](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:659), [env.ts:2](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/env.ts:2).

   **Failure:** Execution captures `full` settings, then awaits readiness/root checks. The agent is restored to its approved policy before authorization. Comparing the current row succeeds, while the prepared argv still uses captured `full`. Independently, unchanged agent/version rows can conceal changed inherited hooks, MCP tools or configuration.

   **Fix:** Build owned argv, cwd, environment and settings from the frozen policy. Authorize the actual prepared invocation’s hash and policy generation. Either freeze/override effective noncredential configuration or include its verified identity in policy; unverifiable reach must block worker readiness.

9. **N9 · P2 — “Use the existing remap flow” is false for policy changes.**

   **Locations:** §§2/12; [coding-agents.ts:163](apps/server/src/http/coding-agents.ts:163), [workflow decisions.ts:224](apps/server/src/domain/workflows/decisions.ts:224), [workflow views.ts:234](apps/server/src/domain/workflows/views.ts:234).

   **Failure:** Coding permission/workspace updates do not change lifecycle incarnation. Remap rejects `untracked` and unchanged incarnations; the UI likewise suppresses the remap item. A safely blocked “agent changed” worker therefore has no promised recovery action. Already-returned queued handles also fail the existing provably-unsent remap predicate.

   **Fix:** Introduce a versioned worker-policy identity into mappings, gates, remap plans and journal verification. Establish no-launch proof before recreating queued work. Reapproval must bind the new policy, payload and reach acknowledgement; changed reach cannot inherit consent merely from the old boolean.

10. **N10 · P2 — Post-handle refusals cannot reuse the existing generation machinery safely.**

    **Locations:** §§4/9; [executor.ts:2076](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2076), [ops.ts:347](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/ops.ts:347), [service.ts:352](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:352), [executor.ts:2059](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2059).

    **Failure:** Readiness/policy refusal arrives after the handle made the attempt `running`; `recordSendFailure()` ignores it. Simply invoking `recordRefusal()` leaves the old run/message linkage and accepted turn intent. Redispatch with the same turn returns the old run immediately. Moreover, every returned handle resets `refused_streak`, preventing the intended five-consecutive-refusal pause for queued refusals.

    **Fix:** Define a durable asynchronous no-launch receipt keyed by attempt, dispatch generation and launch ID. Give runtime admissions generation-qualified identities, reconcile message linkage, and reject stale callbacks/results by generation. Reset refusal streak on qualified release, not handle return.

11. **N11 · P2 — Recovery and revocation have contradictory retry/disposition rules.**

    **Locations:** §§4/6/9; [executor.ts:2250](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2250), [planner.ts:229](apps/server/src/domain/workflows/planner.ts:229), [7.1 waiting rules](docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:115).

    **Failure:** §6 makes an unreleased recovered launch `failed/runtime`, which existing reconciliation accepts as a failed try and auto-retries. §9 says it consumes no logical try. Also, §4 sends **every** authorization “no” through refusal generations, including Stop and temporary holds. That can count ordinary Pause/Resume cycles toward the refusal lifetime limit, contrary to 7.1.

    **Fix:** Define separate transitions for proven readiness refusal, policy-remap blockage, temporary waiting and Stop cancellation. Recovery must use the same classification. Proven no-execution carries known-zero execution cost and must not become an ordinary model-failure token.

12. **N12 · P1 — W’s active Stop and the claimed shutdown barrier remain unsafe assumptions.**

    **Locations:** §7; [service.ts:474](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:474), [executor.ts:2407](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2407), [executor.ts:316](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:316), [start-server.ts:173](apps/server/src/start-server.ts:173).

    **Failure:** Active Stop returns `stopped` while termination is still pending; a probe confirmed this. The executor acknowledges it immediately. Existing Stop delivery remains sequential, and `close()` awaits only the pump. Adding awaited Stop jobs without changing shutdown ordering can recreate the old cycle: executor waits for queued runtime completion before `coding.close()` cancels that queue.

    **Fix:** Make coding `stopped` mean qualifying end/no-launch proof; pending delivery returns `unconfirmed`. Introduce tracked asynchronous Stop jobs and initialize queue cancellation/runtime shutdown before awaiting them. Keep ingest/database services available until their receipts commit.

13. **N13 · P1 — Late proof needs independent result eligibility; W does not publish it.**

    **Locations:** §6; [service.ts:835](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:835), [translate.ts:326](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/translate.ts:326), [executor.ts:2240](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2240), [executor.ts:2250](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2250).

    **Failure:** Background reaping deletes the record but produces no proof observation, leaving executor obligations unresolved. Ordinary terminal ingestion refuses to update an already-terminal run. Conversely, merely promoting an unconfirmed completed run to `'runtime'` makes an `unknown` attempt enter normal acceptance and consume its old result. Only `abandoned` currently has nonconsumable late evidence.

    **Fix:** Store monotonic execution proof separately from disposition and result eligibility. Once unconfirmed, its old result stays ineligible after late proof. Atomically publish proof, close Stop/capacity obligations, settle accounting and delete the process record. Apply this to normal completion, recovery and background reaping.

14. **N14 · P1 — Reservations do not enforce coding minutes or deadlines across process death.**

    **Locations:** §8; [process.ts:148](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:148), [service.ts:632](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:632), [service.ts:869](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:869).

    **Failure:** Reserve ten minutes, release, then kill Score. Its timer disappears while detached work continues. Keeping a ten-minute reservation does not bound an hour of execution. Moving the timer into the launcher alone fails when that launcher is killed. After restart, observing `gone` also does not reveal the actual end time, so “settle to measured time” can refund usage without evidence.

    **Fix:** Persist start/boot identity and absolute expiry, and specify a deadline mechanism that survives the stated supervisor failures. Settle measured time only from a durable end receipt; otherwise charge conservatively and expose possible overrun. If offline enforcement is unavailable, explicitly weaken the guarantee rather than presenting minutes as a hard execution bound.

15. **N15 · P1 — A dropped Codex final message can promote commentary into an authoritative verdict.**

    **Locations:** §10; [process.ts:172](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/process.ts:172), [codex.ts:79](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/codex.ts:79), [codex.ts:100](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/codex.ts:100).

    **Failure:** A mocked child emitted preliminary `agent_message` text containing `passed`, then a final `changes_requested` message over the 1 MiB line cap, then `turn.completed`. The actual reader/parser returned the preliminary text, `result.ok=true`, `groupGone=true`, and `malformed=1`. Selecting the last **observed** message on completion still accepts the preliminary verdict.

    `turn.completed` carries usage, not final text or an authoritative final-message identifier. Upstream’s JSONL definition confirms that shape; this is not an attestation of the pinned CLI version’s stronger guarantees. [Codex JSONL source](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/main/codex-rs/exec/src/exec_events.rs).

    **Fix:** Require a tested authoritative final-output channel, or a validated loss-free protocol that proves which message is final. Dropped/malformed records that could contain the final answer invalidate result eligibility. Never fall back to earlier commentary.

16. **N16 · P2 — Runtime-scoped unknown-cost acceptance cannot be represented by today’s aggregate.**

    **Locations:** §8; [budget.ts:124](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/budget.ts:124), [executor.ts:1996](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:1996), [planner.ts:463](apps/server/src/domain/workflows/planner.ts:463), [translate.ts:281](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/translate.ts:281).

    **Failure:** Accept Codex uncertainty but leave Hermes uncertainty unaccepted. The current aggregate cannot distinguish them. Converting that acceptance into the existing global boolean exempts Hermes too; retaining the global false blocks accepted Codex uncertainty. With no dollar budget, current code does neither pause. Usage also remains transient until termination.

    **Fix:** Preserve uncertainty provenance per attempt/runtime and evaluate only unaccepted scopes. Define unbudgeted-run behavior explicitly. Journal the complete scoped authorization state and persist observed usage/provenance at the writer so restart cannot silently reclassify it.

17. **N17 · P2 — New results and launch/accounting records escape the storage inventory.**

    **Locations:** §§4/8/10/11; [verdict.ts:100](apps/server/src/domain/workflows/verdict.ts:100), [executor.ts:2370](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:2370), [planner.ts:219](apps/server/src/domain/workflows/planner.ts:219), [prune.ts:57](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/prune.ts:57).

    **Failure:** §10 adds retained `wf_results` output, then the planner copies it into node output. Current acceptance charges step/message/run text, excluding those copies. Charging thinking/tools up to the ordinary allowance therefore still understates storage and can consume the protected reserve later. Launch rows, refusal receipts and debit/settlement rows add another unpriced inventory.

    **Fix:** Extend reserve formulas and writer-side charging to every retained copy and generation record, using actual byte deltas. Include all new records in bounded retention and atomic pruning; release charges only when associated records are deleted. Reserve proof/Stop/accounting receipt capacity independently of ordinary observations.

18. **N18 · P2 — Removing an unconfirmed worker changes its remaining Stop routing to Hermes.**

    **Locations:** §§1/6/7; [coding-agents.ts:197](apps/server/src/http/coding-agents.ts:197), [service.ts:126](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:126), [coding port.ts:84](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/port.ts:84).

    **Failure:** An unconfirmed run has terminal status, so the deletion busy check permits removing its agent despite a surviving process record. The composite port then stops recognizing its ID as coding and routes owed Stops to Hermes. The fake Hermes port returns success without acting on that unknown run at [fake-port.ts:1960](apps/server/src/hermes/fake/fake-port.ts:1960), allowing acknowledgement without coding proof.

    **Fix:** Route Stop/recovery by durable run/launch runtime identity, including removed agents. Prevent removal while unresolved execution exists, or retain a tombstoned runtime identity that continues servicing obligations.

19. **N19 · P3 — The room may display commentary after accepting a different final answer.**

    **Locations:** §10; [message-runs.ts:568](apps/server/src/domain/message-runs.ts:568).

    **Failure:** A distinct authoritative final reply is recorded for the workflow, but ordinary completion preserves differing streamed text unless held text was lost. The room can show the preliminary verdict while the workflow follows the final verdict.

    **Fix:** Give owned coding replies separate commentary/final parts, or replace their displayed answer with the accepted authoritative result. Charge the resulting retained copies consistently.
