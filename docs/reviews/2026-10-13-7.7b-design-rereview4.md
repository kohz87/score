New findings:

1. **P2 — Re-gating a suspended attempt can reserve the step twice (§§6,12a).**  
   [budget.ts:110](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/budget.ts:110) counts a `created` attempt’s reservation **and** its step’s estimate when `pending_gate` or `queued`. [executor.ts:1629](apps/server/src/domain/orchestration/executor.ts:1629) checks that total before admission.  
   **Scenario:** a suspended attempt retains a $1 reservation; remap opens its new gate under a $1 remaining budget. Accounting becomes $2. Approval leaves it `queued`, so normal re-admission pauses for budget indefinitely.  
   **Fix:** define exactly one reservation owner throughout suspension, re-gating and re-admission—either retain it on the attempt and exclude the step’s duplicate, or transfer it atomically. Test a tight-budget remap across restart.

2. **P2 — Expiry can pass between authorization and actual spawn (§§6,9).**  
   Release performs journal durability and projection work before `go` ([unit-backend.ts:1112](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/unit-backend.ts:1112)). The supervisor’s [go handler](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/supervisor.mjs:661) starts the CLI without checking expiry; deadline enforcement uses a periodic timer.  
   **Scenario:** authorization succeeds just before the deadline; commit or socket delivery crosses it. An expired `go` arriving before the next timer tick still starts the CLI. A mocked probe confirmed this.  
   **Fix:** require the supervisor to check absolute expiry in the `go`/spawn path, immediately before spawning. Test delayed delivery and receipt-writing delays. Once `releasing` is durable, preserve `mayHaveExecuted` and its step charge; do not manufacture a pre-release revocation.

3. **P3 — §0 still misdescribes where runtime identity exists (§§0,2).**  
   `runs.runtime` already exists ([0047_coding_agents.sql:29](apps/server/migrations/0047_coding_agents.sql:29)) and coding admission writes it before queueing ([service.ts:511](apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:511)). Queued and process-group runs have this identity without any launch row. The previous review missed this qualification.  
   **Scenario:** treating §2 as introducing the column prompts a duplicate migration or unnecessary backfill through launch rows.  
   **Fix:** describe `runs.runtime` as existing; the new work is routing controls through it, with launch metadata as recovery fallback.

Every other §0 claim checked matches current code. Probes used memory and mocked external boundaries; no files changed or live CLIs invoked.
