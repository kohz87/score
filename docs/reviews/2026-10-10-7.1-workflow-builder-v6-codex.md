### [high] The restore witness can roll back with the history it protects

**Where:** [Restore quarantine](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:7), [backup guidance](/home/raven/score/docs/SETUP.md:238).

**What happens:** A backup contains an unsent intent and counter *n*. Hermes subsequently accepts its request. Restoring all of `$SCORE_HOME` restores both the database and `workflow-epoch` to *n*: no mismatch appears, and the old absence of `sendStarted` can authorize replacement execution.

A database-only restore can also erase a Stop committed after the last admission without changing the highest dispatch counter. Recovery can then consume a completion and launch successors despite that Stop.

**Why:** “Outside the database” does not mean outside the restore operation. SETUP recommends backing up `$SCORE_HOME`. Moreover, a dispatch counter witnesses sends, not subsequent authorization changes. A restored snapshot may omit entire externally active runs, leaving nothing in the proposed per-run quarantine list to review.

**Change:** Specify the witness’s restore assumptions and a mandatory restore protocol. Untrusted restoration must impose a persistent global admission fence, including when the restored database contains no quarantinable runs. Preserve enough independent history to identify missing admissions and lost fences, or require explicit operator reconciliation. Test whole-home restoration, omitted runs, and restoration after Stop with an unchanged dispatch counter.

### [high] A frozen toolset does not establish the promised absence of background work

**Where:** [Warning removal](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:23), [execution-policy proposal](/home/raven/score/docs/proposals/hermes-run-execution-policy.md:6), [Hermes post-turn review](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/agent/turn_finalizer.py:769).

**What happens:** The proposed capability becomes available and Score removes the acknowledgement. With memory or skill tools enabled, Hermes can still launch a background review that spends tokens and changes persistent state after the main run returns.

**Why:** This review is automatic runtime behavior, independent of the delegation toolset. Hermes [starts a daemon review thread](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/run_agent.py:820); a per-run toolset list does not disable that mechanism. Toolset enforcement and execution-lifetime enforcement are separate guarantees.

**Change:** Keep the acknowledgement until a tested capability also excludes or accounts for automatic reviews and other asynchronous runtime work. Extend the proposal accordingly, or describe `run_tool_policy` solely as tool restriction. Today’s acknowledgement is an acceptable degradation; its proposed removal is unsupported.

### [medium] Human resolution has no defined effect on unresolved obligations

**Where:** [Quarantine decisions](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:11), [unknown obligations](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:29), [replacement barrier](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:98).

**What happens:** A normal Stop produces `cancelled`, which v6 does not accept as termination evidence. Such an attempt can remain unknown permanently. Repeated stopped runs can exhaust capacity unless “the person resolves it” releases obligations—but that release has no specified semantics.

**Why:** In current Hermes, an interrupted worker result becomes [`cancelled`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/platforms/api_server_runs.py:181). It need not later become `completed` or `failed`. V6 correctly says a human assertion is not termination evidence, while retaining barriers and reservations until termination or quiescence.

**Change:** Define an explicit administrative abandonment outcome, distinct from proven termination. Specify permanent admission retirement, prohibition on consuming late results, owed-stop handling, capacity and reservation treatment, ownership guards, and retained tombstones. Any waiver permitting new work alongside unresolved execution must be a separate recorded risk acceptance. Otherwise state clearly that resolution does not release those resources.

### [medium] Closed compression sessions do not prove settled spend

**Where:** [Settlement rule](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:34), [lineage reader](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/hermes/live/recover.ts:40), [cost reader](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/hermes/live/sessions.ts:108).

**What happens:** A partial lineage can still become apparently complete accounting. Healthy completed runs may remain provisional because their session remains open. Conversely, closing the known sessions does not establish that all attributable costs have arrived.

**Why:** `compressionLineage()` still silently stops at ambiguity, errors, and twenty hops. Hermes distinguishes turn completion from session cleanup; [run retirement removes control references](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/platforms/api_server_runs.py:523) without establishing session closure. Background-review costs are recorded separately in [`session_model_usage`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/agent/background_review.py:797), which Score’s session-cost reader does not include.

**Change:** Define adapter facts for qualified ownership, lineage completeness, observation freshness, cost scope, and settlement. Reconcile auxiliary usage without double counting. In unrestricted mode, either label the number as observed main-loop spend or preserve unknown total spend under the chosen policy. Reconcile the retained reservation-release rule with accounting settlement; an `ended_at` predicate cannot supply that guarantee.

### [medium] Root-session filtering silently drops part of an attempt’s replay

**Where:** [Replay rule](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:36), [frame storage](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/domain/watch-frames.ts:283), [Watch response](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/http/watch.ts:73).

**What happens:** After compression rotates the session ID, later frames match neither the attempt’s root ID nor the “without a session ID” category. They disappear from the attempt replay. Other Watch fields can still show another run’s markers, browser context, gaps, or live state.

**Why:** Current frames do not durably store producer session identity. Current Watch queries remain agent-scoped. Equality with the starting session also does not address Hermes’s compression continuations.

**Change:** Persist qualified producer/session ownership and apply one scope to every replay field and stream update. Include proven compression continuations. Alternatively, explicitly offer starting-session-only replay and show the omitted continuation as a visible gap. Evidence without any proven run association belongs in an unassociated feed, rather than implying association with a particular run.

### [medium] The promised control matrix is still absent

**Where:** [V6 controls](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:38), [Team Resume](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/http/team-pause.ts:96), [current Now contract](/home/raven/score/docs/API-CONTRACT.md:439).

**What happens:** Legacy controls can strand or bypass executor holds. Summary generation can admit undeclared work. Guards tied to `finished` can disappear while external execution remains unknown.

**Why:** An ownership error and expected revision do not define control behavior. Team Resume directly clears brief flags; [summaries reserve ordinary runs](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/domain/room-summaries.ts:155); [MCP room posts](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/mcp/tools.ts:123) provide another mutation path. The contract still lacks workflow decisions, quarantine states, lifecycle mappings, and the asserted matrix.

**Change:** Supply the matrix and contract shapes before implementation, as AGENTS requires. Cover team/agent holds, message Retry/Stop, summaries, MCP mutations, room changes, and Flow/brief controls. Define guards using ownership and unresolved obligations, together with the explicit abandonment policy.

### [medium] Attempt reservations leave associated growth and retention undefined

**Where:** [Quota reservation](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:41), [retained retention rule](/home/raven/score/docs/briefs/7.1-workflow-builder.md:78), [independent frame pruning](/home/raven/score/apps/server/src/hub/retire.ts:96).

**What happens:** Reserved attempt rows fit while rooms, messages, intent payloads, transitions, definitions, frames, or media exceed the supposed quota. Independent cleanup can still remove evidence belonging to unresolved attempts.

**Why:** V6 does not define what the 200 MB measures or what the 64 KB reservation covers. Existing Watch cleanup deletes seven-day-old frames independently. “Never pruned” needs to cover associated evidence explicitly, and ninety-day retention still lacks a persisted origin and cleanup rules.

**Change:** Define quota scope, atomic reservations, bounded ongoing writes, and protected space for terminal/Stop records. Specify evidence pinning, retention from a persisted quiescence or abandonment boundary, associated-data cleanup, and visible replay floors. Test exhaustion during result acceptance and Stop recording.

## The design I would approve

- Retain the Hermes-only, text-only DAG and prohibition on uncertain admission replay.
- Allow acknowledged unrestricted execution today, with completion, Stop, budgets, and replay explicitly scoped to observed execution.
- Keep that acknowledgement until stronger execution-lifetime guarantees are tested.
- Make restore quarantine global and reliable under a documented restore protocol.
- Separate proven termination from administrative abandonment, with exact effects on every obligation.
- Specify complete accounting facts, consistently scoped replay, the control/API matrix, and storage/retention rules.
- Add recorded-fixture tests for these counterexamples before building the editor.
