V2 still has P1 gaps in recovery and rewind preconditions.

1. **P1 — The journal does not cover every crash window.**  
   Design: [Durability, steps 2–4](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:45). Hermes can commit the rewind before Score records `rewound`, leaving `planned`, which startup does not handle. Dispatch can likewise succeed before `dispatched` is recorded. The promised command-result lookup does not exist: the [broker](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/hermes/bridge/rewind-broker.ts:41) and [plugin result cache](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/hermes-plugin/score_bridge/commands.py:105) are volatile, and [existing holds expire on restart](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/room-rewinding.ts:53).

   **Change:** Specify recovery for **every nonterminal phase**, before routing or intent recovery starts. Persist the resolved session, expected source/pre-turn row identities, stable command and admission identities, and link the operation to existing run intents. Restore holds from unfinished operations without expiry. Reconcile exact transcript states; counts alone are insufficient. Define the coding path explicitly, without a rewind phase.

2. **P1 — A separate pending-write probe leaves the destructive race open.**  
   Design: [Eligibility, Hermes preconditions](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:37). After `transcript_pending` returns zero, another surface can enqueue a write. Rewind can then [clear that pending queue](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/hermes-plugin/score_bridge/hermes_private.py:339), losing an unpersisted message; [disk-spooled writes](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/session_transcript.py:323) can instead replay afterward. Also, the bridge [recomputes and overwrites the requested turn count](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/hermes-plugin/score_bridge/hermes_private.py:310): a new external turn arriving after eligibility can be rewound too.

   **Change:** Make these **rewind-command preconditions**, checked under the same drain lock as mutation: exact source turn, single-user-turn boundary, expected active rows/prefix, compression state, and all pending queues/spools/reroutes. Use Hermes’s transaction guards to reject changed state. Refuse shared sessions or unsupported pending-state inspection where that proof cannot be obtained.

3. **P1 — Synced group delete inherits partial application from 5.33.**  
   Design: [“never partially”](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:30) and [`kind: 'forget'`](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:60). Builder can rewind successfully, then Reviewer refuses or times out. Score leaves both replies visible although Builder has forgotten its turn. This is explicitly [existing tested behavior](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/test/http/rewind.test.ts:323), not an atomic operation.

   **Change:** For the narrow v2, restrict synced delete to one eligible Hermes respondent. Otherwise journal each affected session separately, expose partial application, and retain the hold until all sessions and Score reconcile. Do not describe a generic bridge `failed` as “nothing changed”: [verification can fail after mutation](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/hermes-plugin/score_bridge/hermes_private.py:314). Mixed coding-agent turns must refuse before any session changes.

4. **P2 — The room hold is not yet a complete write or consumption fence.**  
   Design: [latest causal work and hold](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:32). Existing [message deletion](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/http/rooms.ts:364) and [plan start](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/http/plan-start.ts:147) do not consult the rewind hold. A second tab can delete the question or start its plan while rewind awaits Hermes. Checking again at dispatch discovers this after the session has already changed. A completed plan start can also consume the reply without creating a newer room message.

   **Change:** Enumerate and fence all relevant mutations inside their writing transactions, including deletes, summary changes, plan starts and compaction. Treat persisted plan consumption as a permanent eligibility blocker. Define how the operation’s own admission passes its hold—the [normal dispatcher skips held rooms](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/routing-dispatch.ts:108).

5. **P2 — Active Hermes-turn accounting remains unspecified.**  
   Design: [turn-count eligibility](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:39) and [alternatives model](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:63). After A → B, retaining both terminal `message_runs` without updating active membership makes [rewind counting](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/versions.ts:179) report two turns while Hermes holds one. The next Retry or synced delete fails with `turn_count_mismatch`.

   **Change:** Commit retirement of the exact old run’s transcript membership with the verified `rewound` checkpoint. Preserve its execution, approvals and costs. Distinguish attempts that actually persisted a Hermes user turn from failures before that write, and define logical turn IDs separately from per-attempt admission IDs.

6. **P2 — “Frozen admission input” needs a complete attachment representation.**  
   Design: [same-context regeneration](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:11). Existing `admission_input` freezes text; [dispatch rereads image IDs](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/routing-dispatch.ts:172), and [input construction skips missing images or changes omission notes](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/hermes/live/attachments.ts:7). Retry can rewind a valid answer and then send different attachment context.

   **Change:** Persist the actual admitted attachment selection and final input representation, retain immutable media with hashes, and verify availability **before rewind**. A same-context Retry must refuse missing admission material instead of using ordinary attachment omission behavior. Coding retries also need a persisted required resume ID and continuity flag; a missing ID must not become a fresh session.

7. **P2 — Separate visibility markers need explicit projection and history rules.**  
   Design: [Data model and contract](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:62). Setting `alternative_hidden_at` alone leaves A in [agent context](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/message-runs.ts:765), search and exports, whose current predicates only test deletion. The [FTS trigger](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/migrations/0033_conversations.sql:36) does not observe the new marker. Existing [share-change detection](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/shares.ts:164) also misses replacement.

   **Change:** Specify one normal-visibility predicate and its application to context, summaries, search/index maintenance, exports, Now, unread counts, MCP previews and new shares. Define historical alternatives/version membership separately, including explicit deletes. Preserve frozen shares, but mark them changed. Require integration checks covering repeated Retry, deletion, rewind/version reads and reload.

Direct answers to the design’s questions:

- **Latest causal work complete and serialized?** Not yet. The listed queues, hand-offs, summaries and uncertain approval outcomes are appropriate; plan consumption, compaction and the complete transactional write fence remain missing.
- **Journal sufficient across restarts?** No. `planned`, dispatch uncertainty, terminal recovery and durable command-result reconciliation need the rules in finding 1. No hold may disappear merely because its command expired.
- **Does 5.33 provide the needed guarantees; are the added checks available?** Its [Hermes rewind mutation has atomic active-row and lease checks](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:1826). Raw and repaired conversation APIs support checking turn boundaries. Those checks must be tied to the expected Score turn. There is no existing complete `transcript_pending` API; inspecting one in-memory queue is insufficient.
- **Visibility independent of deletes and versions everywhere?** The separate marker is structurally correct. End-to-end independence still requires the projection and historical-membership rules above.

Against v1: removing replacement resolves findings **#2–3**; coding continuation removes the unsupported fork promise in **#6**; persistent memory/tool effects address **#9**; the complete newest-message event and snapshot watermark address the main issue in **#10**. Findings **#1, #4–5, #7–8** remain partially addressed, as detailed above.

Read-only browsing with an explicit newest-alternative notice is sound for Hermes.  
Coding continuation accurately matches the three implemented CLI resume paths.  
Keeping every execution, approval, Watch frame and Report is correct.  
Private calls remain scoped to `hermes_private.py`, and mutations remain inside Hermes-owned APIs.
