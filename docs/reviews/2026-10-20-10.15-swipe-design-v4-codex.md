**Not approved as P1-free.** V4 still has one P1 recovery gap. Its fail-closed capability gate is sound and currently disables Hermes Retry/Forget on this clone.

1. **P1 — Transcript evidence cannot establish that an uncertain admission has stopped.**  
   Design: [S3–S4](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:172).

   **Failure:** Hermes accepts B, but Score loses its run handle. After restart, an unchanged baseline triggers S3’s “input never persisted” outcome, or identified partial rows trigger its failed-attempt outcome. Neither proves that B cannot subsequently execute or append more rows. Hermes accepts before launching its worker ([source](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/platforms/api_server_runs.py:718)), and explicitly permits nonterminal runs to outlive retention ([source](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/platforms/api_server_run_idempotency.py:169)); Score nevertheless refuses admission replay after 23 hours ([source](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/hermes/live/intents.ts:89)). Releasing the hold from that snapshot can leave a failed alternative on screen while B later changes the session and performs tools.

   **Change:** Require independent proof that the admission is terminal or fenced **before** classifying its final transcript. An absent handle plus baseline/partial rows must remain `unresolved`. Specify a receipt lookup or verified cancellation/quiescence path, and atomically prevent the linked intent from being dispatched after finalization. Apply this requirement to automatic resolution as well as manual Release.

2. **P2 — S2 assumes session exclusivity that routing does not track.**  
   Design: [S2](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:162).

   **Failure:** The claimed “continued outside” knowledge is actually the imported-room continuation recorded in [`room_sessions`](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/domain/outside-sessions.ts:178). It does not establish that a Score-created session has never been resumed elsewhere. Score itself offers its session through [Watch’s terminal resume command](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/web/src/routes/watch/watch-model.ts:235). Another surface can write between S2’s baseline read and admission. S3 may detect the extra rows afterward, but the replacement can already have acted on unintended context.

   **Change:** Define enforceable session exclusion or expected-baseline validation at Hermes admission, including the interval between validation and history loading. Specify how external continuation is detected durably. Where that proof is unavailable, keep same-context regeneration unavailable.

3. **P2 — Coding regeneration lacks a finalization rule compatible with S3.**  
   Design: [R1’s coding branch](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:112), [R6](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:140), and [S3](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/docs/briefs/10.15-swipe.md:172).

   **Failure:** S3 requires every regeneration to finish from a bridge transcript checkpoint, but the coding drivers expose session IDs and execution events, without that Hermes transcript evidence. Existing [coding recovery](/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.15-design/apps/server/src/runtimes/coding/service.ts:2001) interrupts runs without reconstructing their CLI conversations. Applying S3 literally leaves coding operations held indefinitely; bypassing it leaves the remembered-alternative notice undefined after a crash.

   **Change:** Give coding operations explicit completion and recovery rules using the pinned resume ID, authoritative result evidence and process termination evidence. An interrupted attempt whose session contents cannot be verified must display an explicit unknown memory state. Preserve R6’s prohibition on fresh-session fallback.

Against v3: **#1 is resolved** by S1’s actual expected-state mutation. **#5 and #6 are resolved** by the separate Forget machine and staged successors. **#4 now has explicit admission outcomes.** **#2 remains incomplete** through finding 2; **#3’s transcript checkpoint needs the quiescence requirement in finding 1.**

Direct answers to the design’s questions:

- **Latest causal work complete and serialized?** The Score-side enumeration is substantially complete: queues, reservations, frozen summaries, plan consumption and uncertain approval deliveries are covered. R4 and S6 provide the needed transaction boundaries. External session exclusion remains incomplete.
- **Journal sufficient across Score/gateway restarts?** Not yet. Stable identities, exact manifests and non-expiring holds address the earlier crash windows, but findings 1 and 3 prevent a complete recovery argument.
- **Does 5.33 supply the guarantees, and do the named calls exist?** Its mutation supplies useful transactional guards; its existing orchestration is insufficient by itself. [`rewind_to_message(session_id, target_message_id, *, preserve_compaction_handoff=False, expected_active_ids=None, expected_target_content=None)`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:1826) exists with exactly those parameters and checks them inside its transaction. [`get_messages_as_conversation`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:1512) supports raw/repaired projections, inactive rows and physical row IDs. [`get_messages(include_inactive=True)`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:1384) exposes archive flags, and [`get_active_message_ids`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/hermes_state_messages.py:1797) supplies the complete physical active set. Complete read-only pending-spool inspection remains unavailable: [`drain_transcript_spool`](/home/raven/reference/hermes-agent/gateway/shutdown_flush.py:135) mutates files and can report zero after a scan failure. **S1 therefore correctly requires unavailability on this clone.**
- **Visibility independent of deletes and versions?** Yes at the design level. R7 covers normal reads, FTS, exports, summaries and shares, with separate historical predicates. Its integration tests are appropriate.

Read-only older alternatives with an explicit memory notice are sound.  
The three coding resume paths support the continuation-only approach; disabling their fresh-session fallback is correct.  
Preserving executions, approvals, costs, Watch history and persistent tool/Knowledge effects is correct.  
S1 keeps private calls in `hermes_private.py` and mutations inside Hermes-owned APIs; no bridge-owned database writes are needed.
