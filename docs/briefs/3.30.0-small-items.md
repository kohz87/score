## Items (files under `apps/web/src/bold/` unless noted)
1. **Tasks: the New task dialog traps focus** (`BoldTaskEdit.tsx` NewTaskDialog): a native `<dialog>` opened with
   `showModal()` (inert background, focus trap), Escape closes, focus returns to "+ New task". Same for any other Tasks
   dialog that lacks it (Archive confirm, Edit) — check.
2. **Tasks: on a phone a task opens as a bottom sheet**, as mock-up `20-tasks-phone.dc.html` (grip, ×, title, state line,
   the moves in a 2-column grid, Archive…), not full screen.
3. **Recipes: every problem can be seen.** The one-line problems summary keeps "N things to fix before saving: <first>.
   Show it", and gains "Show all", which lists every problem (each with its Show it) under the line; Hide folds it.
4. **Recipes: the library doesn't fetch every recipe at once.** Each card fetches its recipe's detail (for the facts
   line) only when the card scrolls into view (IntersectionObserver; immediately when there is no observer, as in tests),
   and keeps it cached as today.
5. **Custom looks keep the conversation's top fade** (the 22px fade at the top of the stream disappears under custom
   themes; find why — a theme override of the mask/gradient or its token — and make the fade use theme tokens so every
   look keeps it). Check the theme visual spec still passes in your vitest; no baselines.
6. **One job per chat: a chat that still holds several open jobs (from before 3.28.0) shows a job bar too**: the newest
   active job's bar, plus "+N more open" that opens Info › Open jobs. Stop job there ends that job.
7. **"Stopped …" stays after the bar goes**: after Stop job the bar unmounts; the result ("Stopped “<title>”. It's in
   Past jobs.") shows as a `role="status"` line where the bar was, for a few seconds or until the next job starts.
8. **A job waiting on you says so in its bar** even when no needs-you card is in view: the bar shows a protected "Needs
   you" pill (data-protected, on the protected list) while the job's state is `waiting_on_you`, and tapping it scrolls to
   the card when there is one.

(Not built, resolved by one job per chat: "questions anchor to their topic" and "waiting-on-you topics have no card"
