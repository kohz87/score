# 3.25.0 — Results fold into Info (brief)

Owner, 2026-11-04: "do we still need result and workbench under info?" → chose **Fold into Info**: the chat's side
panel is **Info | Graph** as the mock-up (`docs/design/bold/mockups/18-chats.dc.html`); the Results tab and its
Results/Workbench switch go. DECISIONS 161.

## What changes
1. **Tabs.** The side panel's tabs become Info and (when the current topic has a run) Graph. The Results tab and the
   `bold-work-tabs` switch (BoldRoom.tsx ~417–456) are removed.
2. **Files and results in Info.** Info shows a **Files and results** section for the current topic (the one the
   composer posts to; the general conversation too), from the same reads Results uses today (`jobResults`/workbench
   queries, BoldResults.tsx / BoldWorkbench.tsx): each row an icon, the name, who made it and when; drafts, files,
   screens and links as Results lists them now; empty → one quiet line ("Nothing made in this topic yet"). Keep it
   compact (the panel is 300px); more than 6 → "Show all (N)".
3. **The viewer.** Opening a row opens the **Workbench viewer** (BoldWorkbenchView: the file or draft, its comments,
   screens, the protected approval bar on an agent's screen) as a **wide sheet over the conversation** (desktop: over
   the conversation column, the chat list and Info stay; Escape and a close button return focus to the row; phone:
   full screen). Everything the Workbench does today keeps working there, including every `data-protected` surface.
4. **Links keep working.** `?work=results` opens Info at Files and results; `?work=draft…`, `?work=…&file=` and every
   "Open … in the Workbench" / Show in Results link open the viewer sheet on that item; `?work=graph` unchanged. Old
   links never land on a dead tab.
5. **Direct chats** had no Results; they get the same Files and results section in their Info (DirectInfo) for the
   current chat.
