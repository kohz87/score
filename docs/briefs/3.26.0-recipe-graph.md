## The change
1. **A layered view replaces the canvas on the Recipes page.** Lay the definition out top to bottom by rank (longest path
   from the input), never by stored positions (keep the stored positions untouched in the draft so nothing is lost on
   save). Each layer is a row; nodes of one layer sit side by side (parallel steps), with the mock-up's bracket above and
   below and, before a join, a small pill ("both finish, then" / "all N finish, then").
2. **Cards as the mock-up / the job map** (`bold/jobmap/` look): YOU · INPUT card (the recipe's inputs), STEP n ·
   AGENT cards (AgentGlyph + agent colour in the kicker, the step's label, a muted line "Uses: … · N retries · on failure:
   …"), the **approval gate as a light card** ("YOU · APPROVAL", its ports as "Approve → … · Send back → …"), a branch as
   a card whose outgoing paths are labelled by port, Done as the pill. Connectors are 1px lines.
3. **Back-edges** (a "send back" or a loop to an earlier step) are drawn as a labelled return line or a small marker on
   the source card ("↩ back to step 1"), never crossing cards.
4. **Editing:** click/Enter selects a card (amber ring as the mock-up) and opens its settings in the right panel (reuse
   `NodeInspector`: agent, instruction, retries, on failure, plus "Runs after …" for connecting, "Add a step after",
   "Delete step"). A **+ between two layers** inserts a step on that edge (the existing add-after logic). Keyboard: the
   cards are buttons in reading order; Delete/Backspace on a selected card asks before deleting, as today.
5. **Keep** the toolbar (Graph | As text, Undo, Redo, Preview nothing runs, Run…, Export, More, Save as version N), the
   "Looks good"/problems line (clicking a problem selects its card), and Preview's step overlay on the new cards (the
   preview-stepper's states shown on each card). The "Add to the workflow" palette below can go if "+ between layers" and
   the inspector cover adding every node kind (agent step, gate, branch, join); otherwise keep a compact "Add…" menu on
   the "+". Remove zoom and "Arrange automatically" (no free layout).
6. **Phone** (`19-recipes-phone`): full-width cards, parallel steps in two columns, settings as a bottom sheet.
