"Room stage" (decision 110). A local copy of that page is `/home/raven/score-worktrees/10.10-preview.html` (its `luna/`

packs come from `e2e/pet-pack.ts`). Read its `#stage` section's CSS and `stRoom`/`stShow`/`stTick`/`stMood` JS: the
behaviour there is what the owner approved. It also encodes lessons from review, keep them:
- each member's box is `width: max-content` and its images opt out of `max-width` rules, or a narrow anchor squeezes
  the character thin;
- speech bubbles and "!" sit inside the stage (beside the head; on the left near the right edge), never above it;
- a soft shadow under the feet, no floor line;
- members keep apart (about 125 px around a pet, 85 px between marks), checking both where others stand and where they
  are going.

## Behaviour (done when)
1. **Where**: under the room title in Chat (rooms and direct rooms), above the thread. Members = the room's members
   (`RoomSchema.members`, plus the lead if not listed); a direct room has its one agent. Removed agents are left out.
2. **Who is drawn how**: an agent with `style.pack` plays its pack files (`petPackUrl`; same files as `PetAvatar`,
   reuse its loading/crossfade/still-frame code, `idle` fallback with Score's overlays), with its mark (`AgentGlyph`
   `mark`) as a badge and its name below. Agents without a pack are their mark (`AgentGlyph` at a stage size), which hops
   while moving. Colour + shape always identify every member (AGENTS.md).
3. **Moods, from live state in this room** (find the exact sources in `apps/web/src/data/` and Chat):
   - *waiting for you*: the agent has a pending, actionable approval for this room (or, if approvals can't be tied to a
     room, any pending actionable approval) → `needs` file (or idle + "!" overlay), hops, steps to the front;
   - *working*: the agent is producing a reply or running tools in this room (typing event with text, streaming
     message, tool activity) → `work`;
   - *thinking*: the agent has started a turn in this room but nothing is streaming yet (typing without text, plan
     phase) → `think` (or idle + thought dots);
   - *paused / offline*: dimmed; `sleep` if present (or idle + z's);
   - otherwise *resting*.
   Mood changes crossfade; the screen-reader text of each member says its name and mood ("builder, waiting for you").
4. **Resting life** (Settings › Look › Agents, `data/liveliness.ts`): Lively = idle 3–8 s, walk to a clear spot (pack
   `walk`, mirrored when going left; marks hop), sometimes a break (`coffee`/`stretch`/`read` when the pack has them);
   Calm = moods only, no walking or breaks; Still or `prefers-reduced-motion` = still first frames, no motion at all.
   Pause everything when the tab is hidden or the stage is scrolled out of view or hidden.
5. **Hand-offs**: when a message in this room from one member @mentions another, the two face each other for ~4 s.
6. **Click a member** → inserts `@name ` into this room's composer (at the caret, with a space before if needed) and
   focuses it; a small "hi!" bubble beside the head. Keyboard: members are buttons in a labelled group.
7. **Hide stage**: a toggle in the room header area ("Hide stage" / "Show stage", `aria-expanded`), remembered per device
   in `localStorage` (`score.roomStage`, read/write wrapped in try/catch; default shown).
8. **Phones** (< 560 px): a shorter strip; at most 5 members, then a "+N" chip that opens the room's member list.
9. **Theme protection**: the stage, its members, badges and overlays are a protected surface like the glyphs and
   `PetAvatar` (see `themes/guard.ts`, `protected-surfaces.ts`, `styles/protected.css` and their tests). Anything Score
   moves with `transform` (mirroring, hop, step forward) must be exempted the way `.pet-avatar__stage/anim/fx` are, or
   centred/positioned without transforms; the guard test that lists moving parts must stay exact. If any part is
   `position: fixed/sticky`, add it to `KNOWN_OVERLAYS`.
10. **Cost**: pack files are the same immutable URLs as elsewhere (cached once); a 12-member room must stay smooth
    (one rAF loop for the page, not per member).

## Tests (failing first)
Unit: mood mapping from room state (each source), member list (lead, removed agents), pet vs mark rendering and badge,
liveliness modes (no walking under Calm, no animated images under Still / reduced motion), spacing rule, click inserts
the mention, hide persists (and survives a throwing localStorage), phone "+N", protected/guard tests.
E2E (fake mode): a room with a pack agent (generated pack) and marks shows the stage; a fake typing/streaming turn
shows working; a pending approval shows "!" and the member in front; click inserts `@name`; hide stage survives reload;
Still shows no animated `<img>`. Any agent or pack an e2e adds is removed in `finally`.
Visual baselines: the stage changes Chat screenshots. At the end, regenerate only the visual specs that fail because of
the stage (`--update-snapshots=changed`), list every file, and rerun them; the lead reviews before merge.
