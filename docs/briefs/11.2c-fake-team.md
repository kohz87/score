## Build
`apps/server/src/hermes/fake/team-seed.ts`, `seedFakeTeam(sqlite, nowMs)`, called once at startup in fake mode (after
migrations, after the team room exists; idempotent: a second start changes nothing). Agents `lead`, `builder`,
`reviewer`, `designer` (no persona names). With the room lead set to `lead`, seed:
1. **"Activity feed"** job (working, holder builder): the person's request; a builder → reviewer review over 2 rounds
   (send-back "Events in the same millisecond get skipped", then approved), builder's `file` parts (api/events.py,
   api/test_events.py), a browser watch frame (preview) and a terminal frame (screen) on builder's run; a pending
   approval for builder ("npm install ws@8") shown as a needs-you card; lead's "Done. Here it is." reply.
2. **"Release notes 2.4"** job: lead ↔ reviewer review paused at 8 of 8 turns (`paused_budget`, `exchange_budget`,
   held turn), plus designer's draft (a `document` part).
3. **"Queue research"** job (done): lead's summary message.
4. **One-to-ones**: builder's direct room with three chats (titles and first lines as the prototype's `dms.builder`),
   reviewer with one; one chat referenced from the Activity feed job (a `ref` part) and one message shared into it (a
   `shared` part).
5. **A pinned decision** in the team room ("Event cursors are (created_at, id)").
6. Unread: the Release notes job has unread agent messages; Activity feed is read.
Times relative to `nowMs` (minutes ago), so relative times read like the prototype ("just now", "2 min").
