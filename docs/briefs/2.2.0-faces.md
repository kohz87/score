# 2.2.0 Agents with eyes (brief)

Owner, 2026-10-26: "proceed with these" on the avatar mock-up. Decision 127 (docs/DECISIONS.md) is the spec summary.

## The mock-up (source of truth for look and motion)
`/home/raven/score/.scratch/avatars/project/Main.dc.html` and `Avatar.dc.html` (read-only; outside the repo, not
copied in). Read their script: `SHAPES` (silhouettes on a 120 viewBox, with `eyeY`, `gap`, `shift`, `eye {w,h,round,tilt}`),
`avatar(i, mood)` (moods, blink, look-around, pointer follow, `socket()` squircle path), `greet()` (tap → happy hop), the
CSS keyframes (av-breathe, av-hop, av-shake). Rebuild in React; never paste its markup.

Rules the owner set while iterating (all binding):
- Eyes are **blank white sockets**: no pupils, no highlights.
- Sockets differ per shape, are bigger than the concept's, a bit rectangular with rounded corners and gently rounded
  sides (the `socket()` squircle), **straight** (no bend), mirrored tilt per shape where the mock-up tilts.
- thinking = **flat horizontal line eyes** (level, ~5 units tall on the 120 scale, drifting slowly side to side, no blink).
- working = half-lidded (a lid in the body colour over the top ~48%), glancing down, small side-to-side drift.
- needs you = **big round "shocked" eyes** (circles sized to fit the shape) + a small shake.
- done = happy ^ ^ arcs (white strokes), kept apart, + a hop.
- idle = looks around now and then and blinks (the eye squashes; per-agent timing so a team doesn't blink in unison).
- Eyes never poke past the silhouette in any mood.
