# 3.4.0 A second team (design note and brief)

Owner, 2026-10-26: "optional second team", with members picked per team and overlap allowed (answer to the lead's
question: "Pick members, overlap ok"). Decision 136. Amends D14–D16 ("One Score per team"): a Score can now hold more than
one team room on the same machine; a team that works for someone else still gets its own Score/VM (SETUP §6d), because
team rooms in one Score share the agents, their Hermes profiles, memory and credentials.

## Model
- **The first team room** stays as it is: every ready agent is a member (kept in sync), it can't be renamed, archived,
  deleted or given other members, and it is what `/b`, triggers, "Share to the team room" and the first-run lead picker
  mean. It is now marked `everyone: true`.
- **More team rooms** (`role: 'team'`, `everyone: false`): made by the person with a name, the agents they choose (one or
  more, any agents, an agent may be in several teams) and a lead from those members. Everything a team room does works
  in them unchanged: jobs as threads, exchanges routing, the lead by default, needs-you cards, recipes and "lead decides"
  runs started from that room, Results/Workbench, pins, room tools. They can be renamed, archived, deleted (existing
  room rules) and given other members/lead (`PATCH /api/rooms/:id`).
- **An agent that leaves Score** leaves every team room it was in (existing `retireAgentFromRooms`); a second team whose
  lead left has no lead until the person picks one (same as the first team room today). A team room left with no members
  stays, empty, until the person deletes it or adds someone.
- Agents not yet ready (setting up, needs attention) can't be added to a chosen team.

## Server (lead)
- Migration 0091: `rooms.team_everyone INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (team_everyone IN (0, 1))`; the existing team room
  gets 1; the one-team-room index `rooms_team` is replaced by `rooms_team_everyone` (unique where `team_everyone = 1`).
- `teamRoomId()` = the everyone room. `ensureTeamRoom` / `syncTeamRoomMembers` touch only that room.
- `RoomSchema.everyone?: boolean` (sent on team rooms).
- `POST /api/team-rooms { name, members: AgentId[] (≥ 1), leadAgentId }` → `Room` (201). Validates: unique room name,
  members exist and are ready, lead ∈ members. Emits `room.upserted`.
- `GET /api/team-rooms` → `{ items: Room[] }`, the everyone room first, then by name; archived ones included with
  `archived: true` (the switcher hides them behind "Show archived").
- `PATCH /api/rooms/:id`: the rename/archive/members refusal applies only to the everyone room; for a chosen team,
  `members` must stay ≥ 1 and the lead (if set) must stay a member.
- `DELETE /api/rooms/:id`: refused only for the everyone room.

## Web (coder)
- **Team room ▾ menu** (the room title menu): lists the team rooms (the everyone room first, named as today), then
  "New team…". A chosen team opens at `/b?room=<id>` (the existing room parameter), rendered as a team room; the everyone
  room stays at `/b`.
- **New team dialog**: name, member checkboxes (AgentGlyph + name, ready agents only), lead select (from the checked
  members; required), Create. Errors inline. Focus returns to the menu trigger.
- **In a chosen team room**: the ⋯ room tools apply (rename, archive, delete, members, lead — "Team lead…" exists);
  a "Members…" item edits members with the same checklist. The title shows its name; the dock still shows every agent.
- Everything that today checks `role === 'team'` keeps meaning "a team room"; the few places that mean "the first team
  room" (`/b` canonical URL, dropping `?room=` when navigating to it, the phone "Back to jobs" label, framed paths) use
  `everyone`.
- Start a job from a chosen team room starts it there; its lead plans "lead decides" runs.

## Out of scope (later, if wanted)
Triggers and "Share to the team room" choosing a team; per-team rules or budgets; per-team Team page.
