# 3.27.1 — Chat Info sections fold (brief)

Owner, 2026-11-04: "can each section in chat info be collapsible? otherwise there's too many information cluttered
together". Lead's design (owner tests on live; no mock-up for a behaviour on the existing Info design):

1. Every section of the chat Info panel — group chats (`apps/web/src/bold/chats/ChatInfo.tsx`: Members, Workspace,
   This topic, Files and results, Topics) and direct chats (`bold/direct/DirectInfo.tsx`: Agent, Workspace, Chats,
   Bring someone in, and Files and results) — has a heading that is a toggle button (▸ folded / ▾ open, the existing
   kicker style; `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`; Enter/Space), on desktop and in the phone Info sheet.
2. A folded heading shows a short summary after it: "Members · 11", "Workspace · activity-feed" / "· none",
   "This topic · Release notes 2.4", "Files and results · 4", "Topics · 3 open", "Chats · 5", "Agent · builder".
3. Defaults: This topic and Files and results open; Members, Workspace, Topics (and in a direct chat Chats and Bring
   someone in) folded. The person's choices are remembered on this device per section kind (localStorage, wrapped in
   try/catch; without storage the defaults apply).
4. **Never hide a decision.** A section that holds a needs-you or other `data-protected` decision (This topic's
   Needs you pill / report-back / Stop confirm, any future one) shows a protected "needs you" badge on its heading when
   folded, and opens by itself when a decision arrives (it may be folded again by the person after). The badge carries
   `data-protected` and goes on the protected list, protected.css and its test.
5. `?jobs=1` (Info at Topics) and `?work=results` (Info at Files and results) open their section and scroll to it.
6. Keep the panel's tabs, widths and everything inside the sections as they are.

Constraints: follow AGENTS.md; no new dependencies; don't edit docs/API-CONTRACT.md; don't commit. Vitest for the
toggles (aria), summaries, defaults and remembered state, the auto-open on a needs-you decision, the badge, and the
`?jobs=1` / `?work=results` landings; rewrite tests that assumed open sections without weakening. Lint, format,
typecheck, the full apps/web vitest. No e2e, no baselines, except one temporary look-check spec on port 6483
(production build; never `SCORE_E2E_DEV=1`), dark, 1440 and 390, VIEWED; delete it. Never git stash.
