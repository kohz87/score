# 11.1c: budgets, pause-and-ask, step in (lead)

PLAN 11.1c; design `docs/briefs/11.0-bold.md` §4.5–§4.6; builds on 11.1b (`domain/exchanges/`). Migration 0082.

## Done when (PLAN)
An exchange pauses at 8 of 8 turns with a needs-you card; "Give 4 more", "Let lead decide" (lead extends once, never
as a participant), "Step in" and "Stop" each work (tests); the job-wide 40-turn limit stops nested asks and repeated
hand-offs; spend cap and unknown cost pause before admission; Pause everyone holds exchanges without spending turns;
every row of brief §4.6 ends in a visible card (one test per row).

## Design
- **Held turn.** Whenever Score can't give the next turn of an exchange (a limit, a failed turn, an unreachable agent,
  an asker who can't take the answer), the turn it would have queued is kept on the exchange (`exchanges.held`: agent,
  input, parent message, chain, from) and the exchange stops with `pause_reason`. A decision releases, redirects or
  drops it. Nothing is queued while paused, so nothing spends.
- **Counting.** An exchange's spend = turns started (`turns_used`, counted at admission) + its turns queued. Before
  queuing a turn in an exchange: exchange spend < `budget`; job spend (every agent-started exchange turn of the job,
  queued, running or finished) < `job_turn_limit`; with `spend_cap_usd`: settled cost of the job's runs + 0.50 per
  running turn < cap, and no finished run of the job has an unknown cost. A child `ask` gets `min(job budget, parent's
  remaining)`; none left pauses the parent instead. Person-started turns never count.
- **Reasons and options** (`Exchange.pause = { reason, options } | null`):

  | reason | state | options |
  |---|---|---|
  | `exchange_budget`, `job_limit` | `paused_budget` | `more_turns {turns 1–20}` · `lead_decides` (not when lead is in the exchange or already extended it) · `step_in {text}` · `stop` |
  | `spend_cap`, `cost_unknown` | `paused_budget` | `more_spend {usd}` (cap) / `continue` (unknown) · `step_in` · `stop` |
  | `turn_failed`, `unreachable` | `waiting_on_you` | `retry` · `reassign {to}` · `stop` |
  | `asker_gone` | `waiting_on_you` | `answer_to_lead` · `stop` |
  | `no_decision` | `waiting_on_you` | `ask_again` · `send_back {note}` · `close` |
  | `author_stalled` | `waiting_on_you` | `ask_again` · `close` |

  §4.6 row 3 (queued behind another job's turn) is no pause: `Exchange.queuedBehind = { agentId, since }` while its
  turn waits on that agent's turn in another job of the room (the capsule shows it).
- **Decisions.** `POST /api/exchanges/:id/decision { option, … }` (human guard + idempotency). `more_turns` raises the
  limit that was hit (exchange budget or job limit) and releases the held turn; `step_in` stores the person's message
  in the job and releases the held turn with it appended, raising the exchange budget by one so the turn is not the
  agent's spend; `stop` stops the exchange and its open children (queued turns fail, running ones are stopped through
  the port); `lead_decides` queues one turn for the room lead with the exchange's summary and the actions `extend
  {turns ≤ original budget}` / `stop_exchange {note}` (new `ExchangeAction`s, lead-only, only in that turn); the lead's
  extend releases the held turn and sets `lead_extended`; a lead turn with neither sends the pause back to the person.
  `POST /api/jobs/:id/stop` stops every open exchange of the job and its running turns.
- **Pause everyone / agent pause.** Already hold queued turns before admission (the dispatcher), so no turn is spent;
  covered by a test.
