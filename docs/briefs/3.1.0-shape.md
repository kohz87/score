# 3.1.0 Customizer Shape and glass on the new shell (brief)

Decision 133 (end of docs/DECISIONS.md). The Customizer (Settings › Look › Customize, `apps/web/src/routes/customizer/` + `apps/web/src/themes/` engine) offers Shape options — corner roundness (sharp/soft/round/exact), density, borders, elevation, surface solid/glass — that the theme engine writes as tokens/attributes, but the new shell's CSS (`apps/web/src/bold/*.css`, bold tokens) ignores them, so they do nothing. Make them apply to the new shell's cards, panels, pills and fields within the design's limits: radius scales the bold radius tokens (pills stay pills), glass gives panels a frosted translucent background with backdrop blur (only where a theme picture or ground shows through), border and elevation adjust the panel edges and shadows. **Protected surfaces keep their own fills and contrast** (the guard and protected tests must still pass; glass never makes a decision surface translucent). Live preview in the Customizer shows the effect. e2e: each option changes the computed style of a card/panel in the room and settings, and themes-* safety specs still pass. SCORE_WEB_PORT=5821.
