# 7.5 Orchestrator — design v1 (2026-10-10)

**Goal (owner):** an agentic workflow: the person gives a goal; a lead agent decides the steps at runtime, hands them to
team agents, asks the person when needed, and finishes — instead of a drawn graph (7.1 comes later and reuses this
executor). Score is the referee: every request the lead makes is validated against the run's limits, budget, gates and
permissions.

## Shape: event-driven lead turns (no blocking tools)
- An **orchestration** is a Score-owned run: `{ goal, lead, allowedAgents, budgetUsd?, deadline?, maxSteps (≤ 30),
  maxLeadTurns (≤ 40), maxConcurrentSteps (≤ 3), gatePolicy }`, its own room and brief (owner: `orchestration`), created
  by the person (paired). It starts with a **lead turn**.
- A **lead turn** is a normal Hermes room run of the lead with a structured input: the goal, the plan so far, every step
  with its status and (quoted, sanitized, ≤ 8 KB each) result, pending gates, remaining budget/limits, and the allowed
  agents. The lead answers by calling orchestrator tools (below) and ends its turn. It never waits inside a turn.
- **Score wakes the lead** with the next lead turn when something it asked for resolves: a step finishes or becomes
  unknown, a gate is decided, a deadline/budget threshold is hit — batched (at most one lead turn running; events that
  arrive meanwhile are delivered in the next turn).
- Steps are ordinary Score-started room runs of the target agent (fixed recipient, routing and hand-offs disabled,
  per-attempt session `orch-<run>-<step>-<attempt>`), so Now/Flow/Reports/Watch show them; results are text (7.1 rules).

## Orchestrator tools (MCP, 7.4d server)
`orch.plan(steps[])` (record/replace the visible plan; no execution), `orch.assign(agent, instruction, after?: stepIds)`
→ `stepId` (queued; Score admits it when its `after` steps succeeded and limits allow), `orch.cancel(stepId)`,
`orch.ask(question)` → gate (shown in Now's Needs you; the person answers), `orch.note(text)` (progress line in the
room), `orch.finish(outcome, summary)`. No tool approves anything, reads other agents' sessions, changes settings,
or starts work outside the run.
- **Identity and scope:** the lead's MCP token identifies the agent; a call is accepted only if that agent is the lead
  of an orchestration with a **running lead turn**, and it applies to that orchestration. (Open question for review: the
  same agent busy in another session at the same time — v1 refuses to start an orchestration while its lead has any
  other active run, and holds the lead's other rooms' turns while a lead turn runs.)
- **Validation per call:** agent ∈ allowedAgents and served; workflow eligibility (7.1 v6 tool-policy rule: without a
  per-run tool policy the run needs the person's acknowledgement); instruction ≤ 8 KB, redacted; step and turn limits;
  budget reservation (node estimate or a default) against remaining budget; deadline not passed; run not stopping/
  paused/quarantined. Refusals are returned to the lead as tool errors and shown in the room.

## Safety (from the 7.1 reviews)
- Durable intents owned by the orchestration; one admission authority; `sendStarted` before each POST; `not_dispatched`
  only without `sendStarted`; restore quarantine via the outside epoch counter.
- Termination evidence = Hermes `completed`/`failed` from the agent loop; anything else `unknown` with obligations kept.
- Stop: run fence → no new lead turns or steps; owed stops; "not confirmed ended" shown until evidence.
- Budgets: soft, checked at each admission (lead turns included) with fresh per-attempt spend; reservations; unknown
  spend pauses by default. Deadlines absolute; expiry fences.
- Prompt injection: step results and person answers are quoted data inside the lead's input; the lead's tool calls are
  the only way it acts, and each is validated; a step can't call orchestrator tools (only the lead's token during its
  turn can); gates are human decisions only.
- Limits: as above, plus total stored data per run ≤ 2 MB; retention 90 days for finished runs.

## Product
"New orchestration" (Chat or Flow): goal, lead, allowed agents, optional budget/deadline, gate policy ("ask me before
each step" | "ask only when the lead asks" | "never ask"), the background-work acknowledgement. The room shows the plan,
step cards (agent, status, result), gates inline, notes; Stop / Pause / Resume. Flow shows the run as a live graph from
the recorded steps and `after` edges.

## Out of scope (v1)
Nested orchestrations, file artifacts between steps (text only), Kanban-task steps, coding agents as the lead (they can
be step agents once 7.2's capability matrix says so), editing the plan by hand mid-run (Stop and restart instead).

## Tests
Fake adapter + recorded fixtures, no model calls: the event loop (assign → step → wake → finish), batching of events
into one lead turn, every refusal path, limits, budget reservations and pauses, gates (each policy), Stop during admission
and with late admission, unknown steps, restart recovery and restore quarantine, injection attempts in step results
(text that looks like tool calls stays data), a lead calling tools outside its turn (refused).
