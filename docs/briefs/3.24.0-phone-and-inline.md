ws@8 · Allow · Don't allow"), not in a strip pinned above the conversation. Do that:
- Every needs-you card today in the pinned strip (`room/RoomRequests.tsx`: approvals, paused exchanges, questions, run
  requests, spend/quota guards, retry/remap, report-back…) renders **inline** at its place in the merged stream: after
  the message it belongs to (an approval's `approvalPointerIds` message, a question's message, an exchange's last turn),

- The pinned strip (`.bold-pins`, its "N more below" cue) is **removed**.
- **Never missed:** when any needs-you card is out of view, a protected **jump chip** floats just above the composer
  ("2 need you ↑" / "↓", tap = scroll to the nearest one, focus moves to it). `data-protected`, on the protected list and
  in protected.css, in `KNOWN_OVERLAYS`. The chat list's "needs you" badge and the top bar count stay.

- Keyboard and screen readers: the cards keep their roles/labels; the chip is a button with a clear name.
