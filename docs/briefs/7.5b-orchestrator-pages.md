# Brief 7.5b: Orchestrator pages (web) — includes the live simulation (owner 2026-10-10)

**Design:** `docs/briefs/7.5-orchestrator.md` (v3 + v3.1) and the stage A contract (API-CONTRACT §14, `packages/contract`).

## Pages

1. **New orchestration** (from Chat and Flow): goal, lead, allowed agents, optional budget/deadline, gate policy, the
   acknowledgement text from the design, and the preflight result (remote mode, lead's MCP access, tool readiness) with
   what's missing.
2. **Orchestration room view:** plan, step cards (agent glyph, status, result preview, full output on open), gates and
   inquiries inline with exact evidence, notes; Stop / Pause / Resume; finish summary; "not confirmed ended" states;
   reconciliation screen when the global fence is up.
3. **Now:** gates, inquiries, budget pauses and reconciliation as typed "Needs you" items.

## Live simulation (owner request)

- **Live diagram** on the Flow canvas: a lead node plus one node per step (agent colour + shape via AgentGlyph), edges
  from `after` dependencies and `replaces` lineage (dashed). Node states: queued (dashed outline), gate (amber, "needs
  you"), admitting/running (pulse), succeeded (solid), failed (red), cancelled (struck), unknown (grey "?"), abandoned.
- **Moving dots:** when an assign is accepted a dot travels lead → step; when a step result is accepted a dot travels
  step → lead; a lead wake lights the lead node. Dots are driven only by real stream events in order, paced to ≥ 300 ms
  each so bursts stay readable, never invented. `prefers-reduced-motion`: no travel animation, the edge/node highlights
  instead. Text alternative: the log below is the accessible equivalent; nodes have names with their state.
- **Live log** beside the canvas (below it on phones): lead notes/plan changes, every orch tool call with Score's verdict
  (accepted / refused + reason), step admissions and results (preview), gate/inquiry decisions, budget/limit/fence
  events, Stop. Click a node → filter the log to that step and open its output. Auto-scroll that pauses when the person
  scrolls up.
- **Replay** of a finished orchestration: a scrubber over the same event history at 1×/4×/16×, play/pause, step
  forward/back; the diagram and log show the state at the scrubbed time (computed from events, not recorded video).

## Done when

diagram reach "succeeded" with log entries, replay it; visual baselines (light/dark/390) for the room view and the live
diagram mid-run, checked by eye. `pnpm check` green.
